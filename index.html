About Mohasorok

মহাসড়ক Mohasorok is a free, fan-made bus driving game set on the Dhaka–Chattogram N1 highway. Everything you see — buses, liveries, roads, towns — is drawn by the game's own code; no images or 3D models from other games or mods are included.

Not affiliated with any bus operator, Hino Motors, or any company. Operator names belong to their owners and are used only to show the buses that run on this road. If you represent an operator and want your name changed or removed, please open an issue on the project page.

Controls (PC): W/S throttle-brake (hold S to reverse) · A/D steer · H horn · Q/E indicators · L lights · X cruise · C or 1–4 camera · V screen mirrors · F refuel · R reset · P pause · M mute. Gamepad supported. Phone: turn the wheel, hold GAS/BRAKE, drag the view to look around.

Credits: 3D engine three.js (MIT). Bangla font Noto Sans Bengali (SIL Open Font License). Bus liveries and shapes painted from public photos (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA/CC0) and the community's reference images. Speeds/distances: N1 compressed 1:12, ~245 km shown.
