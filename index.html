About Mohasorok

মহাসড়ক Mohasorok is a free, fan-made bus driving game set on the highways of Bangladesh: all 64 zillas, from Teknaf to Panchagarh. Everything you see — buses, liveries, roads, towns — is drawn by the game's own code; no images or 3D models from other games or mods are included.

Not affiliated with any bus operator, Hino Motors, or any company. Operator names belong to their owners and are used only to show the buses that run on this road. If you represent an operator and want your name changed or removed, please open an issue on the project page.

Controls (PC): W/S throttle-brake (hold S to reverse) · A/D steer · H horn · Q/E indicators · L lights · U high beam · J fog lights · X cruise · C or 1–4 camera · V screen mirrors · F refuel · R reset · P pause · M mute. Gamepad supported. Phone: turn the wheel, hold GAS/BRAKE, drag the view to look around.

Credits: 3D engine three.js (MIT). Bangla font Noto Sans Bengali (SIL Open Font License). Bus liveries and shapes painted from public photos (Wikimedia Commons, CC BY-SA/CC0) and the community's reference images. Speeds/distances: the road network is compressed 1:12; real kilometres are shown. Road data: towns and highways placed by hand from public maps (approximate).

Import a bus skin

Use your own image: a BUSSID/ETS2 skin template or a side photo of a bus. It stays in this browser only.
Select a region on the right, then drag on the image: drag inside a box to move it, drag its corner square to resize, or drag on empty space to draw it fresh. Turn off Right side to mirror the left side; turn off Front/Rear to use painted ones in the skin's colours.
